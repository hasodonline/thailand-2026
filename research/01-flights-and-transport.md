# Thailand Trip Transport Research Report
## Israeli family of 7 (2 adults + 5 children, ages 10–14) | Sept 18 – Oct 4, 2026

**CRITICAL DATE NOTE:** Sept 18 – Oct 4, 2026 falls exactly across Yom Kippur (Sept 21) and the entire Sukkot holiday (Sept 26 – Oct 3). This is the single busiest outbound-travel window of the year for Israelis. TLV–BKK fares peak in this period and seats on El Al/Arkia sell out months ahead. Book the international legs as early as possible; note also that El Al does not fly Yom Kippur (Sept 20–21) and reduces holiday-eve schedules. September is repeatedly cited as the most expensive month on this route; October is the cheapest — the Oct 4 return leg should be cheaper than the outbound.

---

## 1. TLV → Bangkok (International)

### Direct carriers (as of 2026)
| Airline | Frequency | Aircraft | Duration | Typical times |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **El Al** | ~7–10x weekly (2 flight numbers) | 787 | ~10:45 out / ~11:50 back | LY081 dep TLV ~23:00 (arrives BKK ~mid-day next day); LY083 dep TLV ~01:10 (arrives BKK ~afternoon). LY081 runs ~6 days/wk, LY083 ~4 days/wk |
| **Arkia** | 2x weekly (Mondays + Fridays since Jan 2026) | A330 wide-body | 10:45 out / 11:25 back | First rotation departed TLV 00:05; overnight departures, 2 hot meals included |

- Arkia launched TLV–BKK in Nov 2025, breaking El Al's monopoly; it also flies TLV–Phuket and TLV–Hanoi in 2026.
- Thai Airways is cited as a competitor on the route in Israeli press, but I found no confirmed Thai Airways TLV service for Sept 2026 — treat as unconfirmed.

### Prices (round trip, per person, economy)
- **Baseline direct fares (off-peak):** roughly **$860–$890 RT** (one-way direct from ~$450–490). Arkia one-ways seen from ~$445.
- **September peak / Sukkot:** El Al RT fares seen at **~$1,100–1,200+** for late-Aug/Sept dates. For Sukkot expect **$1,000–1,400 per person** on direct flights; family of 7 realistically **$7,000–9,500 total** on El Al/Arkia unless booked very early.
- Kids 10–14 pay full adult fare (child discounts on El Al are marginal, typically ~10% if any).

### Connecting options (usually cheaper)
- **Etihad (via Abu Dhabi, ~2:45 layover)** and **Emirates/flydubai (via Dubai, ~4:45 layover)** both resumed full Israel–Thailand connectivity. Etihad one-ways run **$400–1,400**; combined RT often **$650–900** in Sept.
- **Azerbaijan Airlines (via Baku)** is regularly the cheapest: aggregators show TLV–BKK one-ways from **~$250** in mid-September, RT from **~$550–770**.
- Total journey time via Gulf: ~14–17 hours vs ~10:45 direct. With 5 kids, the direct overnight flight (sleep on board, land midday) is operationally much easier; the connecting saving is roughly $150–350/person — for 7 people that's $1,000–2,400, so it's a real trade-off worth pricing on your exact dates.
- All direct flights land at **BKK Suvarnabhumi** (not Don Mueang).

## 2. Bangkok → Chiang Mai (Domestic)

Extremely high frequency: 20+ nonstops/day, ~**1h10–1h20** flight time.

| Airline | Bangkok airport | Notes | Typical one-way price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thai AirAsia | **DMK** Don Mueang (some BKK) | Highest frequency | from **THB 900–1,300** ($26–38) |
| Nok Air | **DMK** | Often cheapest, includes 15kg bag on some fares | from ~$42 |
| Thai Lion Air | **DMK** (also flies from BKK) | Cheap, includes bag | RT from ~$81 |
| Thai Vietjet | **BKK** Suvarnabhumi | Convenient if connecting from TLV arrival | from ~$30–40 promo |
| Thai Airways | **BKK** Suvarnabhumi | Full service, 20–30kg bag | ~$60–90 |
| Bangkok Airways | **BKK** | Full service | ~$60–90 |

- **Key logistics point:** If you fly straight after landing from Israel at Suvarnabhumi, choose Thai Vietjet / Thai Airways / Thai Lion from BKK to avoid the 45–90 min cross-town transfer to Don Mueang.
- Budget for 7 pax incl. checked bags: **~THB 10,000–15,000 (~$280–430) one way** on low-cost; roughly double on Thai Airways.
- Late September is low season (green/rainy season) — fares are soft; booking 1–2 months out is fine, but with 7 seats needed, earlier is safer.

## 3. Chiang Mai → Koh Phangan

### Option A: Fly CNX → Surat Thani (URT), then bus+ferry
- **Thai AirAsia is the only airline flying CNX–URT nonstop.** ~10 flights/week (not daily-double), duration **~1h50**. Two rotations observed: **FD5422 dep ~08:25** and **FD5420 dep ~17:30** (days vary — verify your exact date; the evening flight is useless for same-day island transfer).
- Price: one-way from **~THB 1,700–2,200 ($50–65)** per person; ~$62–95 typical.
- **From Surat Thani airport to Koh Phangan (Thong Sala pier):**
  - **Lomprayah** (bus + high-speed catamaran, via Donsak or Tapee): **THB ~1,000 (1,003)** pp, **3h15–4h total**. Airport departures around **09:30 → arrive 12:45** and **12:30 → arrive 16:30**.
  - **Seatran** combo: ~**THB 870**, ~4–5h.
  - **Phantip bus + Raja/Seatran ferry:** ~**THB 669**, 4.5–7h.
  - **Raja Ferry combo:** ~**THB 739**, 4.5–7h15; Raja sails Donsak→Thong Sala at 07:00/10:00/13:00/15:30 (ferry itself 2.5h); last Raja/Seatran ferries from Donsak ~17:30–18:00.
  - **Hard constraint: land at URT before ~14:00** or you risk missing the last combos (arrivals after 16:00 = overnight in Surat Thani). The 08:25 AirAsia flight lands ~10:15 and connects comfortably to the 12:30 Lomprayah.
- **Cost for 7 (Option A):** flights ~$350–450 + Lomprayah ~THB 7,000 ($200) ≈ **$550–650 total; door-to-door ~7–8 hours** (hotel CNX → hotel Phangan).

### Option B: Fly CNX → Koh Samui (USM), then short ferry
- **Bangkok Airways only**, ~14 nonstops/week (2x daily), **~1h45**. Samui airport is owned by Bangkok Airways → premium fares: typically **$130–190 one-way** (lows of ~$78–93 seen in shoulder dates; Sept/Oct is their cheap season). PG242 departs ~11:35 is one daily rotation.
- **Samui → Phangan ferry (to Thong Sala):**
  - **Lomprayah** high-speed cat from **Maenam Pier**: ~**20–30 min**, THB 300–400.
  - **Seatran Discovery** from **Bangrak (Big Buddha) Pier**: ~**30–45 min**, **THB 330**, departures ~09:30 and 14:30 (plus seasonal extras).
  - Samui airport → Bangrak pier is only ~10–15 min by taxi/van (THB 300–500 for a van).
- **Cost for 7 (Option B):** flights ~$950–1,300 + ferry ~THB 2,300 ($65) + taxi ≈ **$1,050–1,400 total; door-to-door ~5–6 hours**.

### Comparison for 7 people
| | Option A (via URT) | Option B (via USM) |
|---|---|---|
| Total cost | **~$550–650** | ~$1,050–1,400 |
| Door-to-door | ~7–8 h | **~5–6 h** |
| Schedule risk | Must catch morning AirAsia flight (not daily — check days) | Daily, more slack |

**Verdict:** Option A saves roughly **$500–750** for the family at the cost of ~2 extra hours — usually the right call for 7 people, provided the morning CNX–URT flight operates on your date. (Third option: fly CNX→BKK→URT with AirAsia if the direct doesn't run that day — often similar price, +2h.)

## 4. Koh Phangan → Bangkok (return)

### Route 1: Ferry/bus to Surat Thani → fly URT → Bangkok (cheapest)
- **Lomprayah** from Thong Sala toward Surat Thani: departures ~**08:00 and 11:00** (cat + bus/van to airport, **3–3.5h** to URT airport); Seatran ferries to Donsak at 05:30/08:30/11:30/14:30/17:30 (2h15 crossing) + 1.5h van to airport; Raja at 05:00/08:00/11:00/14:00/17:30 (2.5–3h crossing). Combo ticket ~**THB 700–1,000** pp.
- **URT → BKK:** ~13 nonstops/day — Thai AirAsia (~45/wk), Thai Lion (~28/wk), Nok Air (~12/wk) all into **Don Mueang (DMK)**; Thai Vietjet into **Suvarnabhumi (BKK)**. ~**1h10–1h15**, one-way from **$25–45 (THB 900–1,600)**.
- Practical timing: 08:00 Lomprayah from Thong Sala → URT airport ~11:30 → any flight from ~13:30 onward. **Door-to-door ~7–8 h** (Phangan hotel → Bangkok hotel).
- Cost for 7: ~THB 6,000–7,000 transfer + ~$220–320 flights ≈ **$400–520**.
- **Important:** if your TLV return departs from Suvarnabhumi the same night, prefer Thai Vietjet URT→BKK (lands at Suvarnabhumi) or leave a big buffer for the DMK→BKK transfer (allow 1.5–2h). Best practice with kids: return to Bangkok a day early.

### Route 2: Ferry to Samui → fly USM → Bangkok (fastest)
- Thong Sala → Samui: Lomprayah (to Maenam) or Seatran Discovery (to Bangrak, ~10:30 and ~16:00 departures), 20–45 min, ~THB 300–400 pp; then 15–20 min taxi to Samui airport.
- **USM → BKK (Suvarnabhumi):** Bangkok Airways ~15–20 flights/day, ~1h05–1h15, one-way ~**$77–115** in late Sept/Oct (Sept is USM's cheapest month). Some Thai Airways codeshares.
- **Door-to-door ~4–5 h.** Cost for 7: ferry+taxi ~THB 3,500 + flights ~$550–800 ≈ **$650–900**.
- Bonus: lands at Suvarnabhumi — same airport as the El Al/Arkia departure.

**Verdict:** Route 1 saves ~$200–350; Route 2 saves ~3 hours and avoids the DMK/BKK airport mismatch before the international flight. For the final connection day, Route 2 (via Samui) is meaningfully lower-risk.

## 5. Car rental for 7 in Chiang Mai

- **Yes, foreigners can self-drive 9-seat vans.** Toyota Commuter (9–12 seats) and Hyundai H-1/Staria are rentable in Chiang Mai by tourists; rental firms explicitly confirm "all customers including Thai and foreigners can hire a van and drive themselves."
- **Companies:**
  - International at CNX airport: **Avis, Budget, Hertz, Sixt, Thrifty, National, Enterprise/Dollar** counters. Budget lists a **Toyota Commuter at ~$40/day**; Toyota Innova (7-seat MPV) from ~$35/day at Budget. Average 7-seater rate in Chiang Mai ~$48–81/day.
  - Reputable Thai chains/locals: **Thai Rent A Car**, **siam.rent / Siam Auto Rent** — self-drive Commuter from **THB 1,980–2,280/day (~$55–65)**, often with delivery to CNX airport.
  - Van **with driver** is a very common alternative in N. Thailand: ~THB 2,500–3,500/day + fuel + driver lodging — worth considering for mountain roads (Pai/Doi Inthanon).
- **Documents (Israelis):** **International Driving Permit is required** — the Israeli license is not in English/Thai, so the license alone doesn't satisfy Thai law (fine THB 500–1,000, and more importantly insurance can be voided). Get the IDP (1949 convention) from **Memsi (משרד הרישוי/ממסי)** before the trip; carry IDP + Israeli license + passport. Driver typically must be 21+ (sometimes 23+) with license held 1+ year; credit-card deposit THB 10,000–20,000.
- **Driving:** **Left side** (like the UK). Roads CNX-area are good; the Commuter is big but manageable. Note Thai law requires seat belts for all passengers.
- **1 van vs 2 cars:** Two compact SUVs (e.g., 2x Toyota Yaris Cross/Corolla Cross at ~$25–40/day each) cost about the same as one Commuter (~$55–80/day total either way), but need **two IDP-holding drivers**. One van keeps the family together and needs one driver; downside is parking size and comfort on curvy roads (rear rows bounce). For 7 people + luggage, the 9-seat Commuter is genuinely the practical choice; a 7-seat Innova/Fortuner will NOT fit 7 people plus 16 days of luggage.

## 6. Bangkok airport (BKK) → city for 7 people

| Option | Price (total) | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2x metered public taxis** | ~THB 800–1,200 total (350–500 meter + 50 THB airport fee + 75–130 tolls, each) | 40–90 min | Simplest at 3am arrival; taxi rank level 1; split 4+3 |
| **Grab (JustGrab/GrabCar) x2** | ~THB 900–1,300 total incl. tolls | 40–90 min | Fixed price shown upfront; pickup level 4 |
| **Grab XL / 6-seater** (seats 6 pax max) | ~THB 600–800 + tolls per vehicle | 40–90 min | **Won't fit 7 — you'd still need a 2nd car**. Base ฿300 + ฿20/km |
| **Pre-booked private minivan (Klook/GetYourGuide/Viator)** — best option | ~**THB 1,100–1,600 ($30–45)** for a 9–10 seat van incl. tolls & meet-and-greet (premium chauffeur services up to $130) | 45–90 min | One vehicle for all 7 + luggage; driver waits with name sign — ideal after the overnight TLV flight |
| **AOT Limousine counter van** (in arrivals hall) | ~THB 1,500–2,200 | 45–90 min | Walk-up, no pre-booking needed |
| **Airport Rail Link** | THB 45 pp = THB 315 total | 26 min to Phaya Thai, then taxi/BTS | Cheapest, runs 06:00–24:00; hard with 7 people + big luggage; no good if landing overnight |

**Recommendation for this family:** pre-book a 9-seat van transfer (~$35–45); it's barely more than two taxis and removes all friction.

### Key booking priorities (date-specific for late Sept 2026)
1. TLV–BKK seats for 7 over Sukkot — book immediately; this is peak-of-peak.
2. CNX–URT AirAsia — only ~10 flights/week; confirm the morning flight operates on your chosen day before locking hotels.
3. Lomprayah — pre-book; the **Oct full moon party is Oct 6, 2026-adjacent** (check exact date) and ferries sell out around it.
4. Late Sept = rainy season on the Gulf coast; seas occasionally cancel speedboats — the big catamarans/car ferries (Lomprayah cat, Seatran, Raja) almost always run.
