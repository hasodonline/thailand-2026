## 2. DUBAI

### 2a. Can Israeli passport holders enter the UAE in 2026? — YES

**Status: visa-free, no pre-approval, no visa on arrival needed.**

Per the **UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Embassy in Tel Aviv** (official mission page, undated but current):
- Under the **Mutual Visa Waiver Agreement**, Israeli citizens with a valid **ordinary passport** need no visa for short visits.

Israeli sources confirm that since **7 December 2024**, the 1-year emergency/temporary Israeli passport requires a pre-arranged visa — only 5-year and 10-year biometric passports get the waiver.

**Action**: verify each of the 5 children holds a standard 5-year biometric passport (not a temporary one) with expiry after April 2027. Children's Israeli passports are 5-year documents, so a passport issued in, say, 2021 will expire in 2026 — check each one individually.

- The same bag bought at the counter = **$90 + €85 ≈ ₪565**.
- **Airport is ~1.9× the price — a ~₪265 penalty per bag round trip.**
- Note the **Larnaca side is charged in EUR at EU-airport rates and is the single most expensive option of all.** Never leave the return-leg bag to Larnaca airport.

**Booking-time vs. later: no difference.** The price depends on **the date you purchase**, not when you booked. Buying today costs the same $50 it would have at booking. But prices have risen three times in 18 months (Jan 2025; 1 Jul 2025 $40→$45; 1 Apr 2026 $45→$50) — **a further hike before September is plausible. Buy now.**

**Do you have to phone 03-7230848? No — and phoning costs money.**
- **Self-service online** via the personal area / **"ניהול הזמנה"** on the Arkia website or app: **no service fee**. Login accepts an SMS one-time code, a password, or **the booking number alone** if no account was created.
- Also purchasable during **online check-in** (opens 24h before, closes 6h before).
- **Phone or travel agent adds a service fee of $10 (1–2 passengers) or $20 (3+ passengers)**, and is only possible up to **48 hours** before departure.
- 03-7230848 is a valid Arkia sales line, but the "you must call" advice would cost you **~$20 (₪60) in avoidable fees**. Try the personal area first. (Arkia also runs WhatsApp service on 03-6903712, Sun–Thu 09:00–17:00.)

**💡 Structural tip: the $70/$90 rates are for a 2nd/3rd bag on the *same passenger name*. Spread bags one-per-person and every single bag is a "first bag" at $50.** Never put two bags on one name.

### What "Fly Lili" is
**Fly Lili is an independent Romanian airline (Bucharest, founded 2021) that wet-leases A320/A321 aircraft to Israeli carriers** — both Arkia and Israir. It is **not** Arkia's low-cost arm, not a subsidiary, not a brand. **Baggage implication: none.** On a wet-lease the flight is sold under Arkia's IZ number and **Arkia's rules and prices apply**; Fly Lili supplies only aircraft and crew. If a Fly Lili-liveried jet shows up at the gate, nothing about your allowance changes.

### Aircraft — not a turboprop
Arkia has retired its ATR/Dash operations. Current fleet: 3 × Embraer E195, 1 × E190, 2 × A321neo LR, A330-200, plus wet-leased A320/A321s. **IZ1161 (TLV→LCA)** has been tracked as **E195**, occasionally A320. **IZ166 (LCA→TLV)** has been tracked as **A320**.
- An **E195 does take a 56×45×25 trolley in the overhead bins**, but it's a 2-2 narrowbody with limited bin volume at 122 seats — **gate-checking of trolleys is a realistic possibility**. Gate-checking a compliant, already-paid-for trolley should be free; the $30 hold charge applies only to oversized/overweight trolleys.
- **Keep passports, medication, phones/chargers, and Thailand-day-one essentials in the 40×30×20 personal item, not in the trolley.**
- *Aircraft assignment for specific Sept/Oct 2026 dates can't be confirmed this far out.*

### ⚠️ The 20 kg vs 30 kg trap
Your Cyprus Airways/flydubai ticket allows **30 kg per person** (CY hold allowance is 30 kg free per adult, max 158 cm; infants 20 kg). **Arkia allows 20 kg.** If you pack to 28–30 kg for the Thailand legs and hand that bag to Arkia:
- **TLV outbound: $10/kg over 20 kg** → a 30 kg bag costs **$100 (~₪300) in overweight on that one leg**.
- **Larnaca return: €10/kg** → **€100 (~₪347)**.

Since a **whole extra 20 kg bag is only $50**, overweight is never the right answer. **Pack to ≤20 kg per bag on the Arkia sectors.** Since you're collecting and re-checking at Larnaca anyway (separate tickets), you *could* redistribute there — but doing that on a hotel-room floor at 07:00 with 7 people is not a plan.

⚠️ **The return is the binding constraint.** Whatever you buy in Thailand comes home through Arkia's 20 kg cap and Larnaca's €85/bag + €10/kg counter prices. **Buy the return-leg bags now, not later.**
