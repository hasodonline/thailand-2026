# OPTIONAL ADD-ONS — KOH PHANGAN (Thong Nai Pan base) + BANGKOK (Chatrium riverside)

*Family: 2 adults + 5 kids (10–14). Verification notes: "Verified" = confirmed on a live page this session; sources noted. Sept 26–Oct 1 is shoulder season — west-coast seas can be choppy, so check conditions for anything snorkel-related.*

## 1. KOH PHANGAN

### (a) Eating IN Thong Nai Pan itself (walk from hotel)
- **Nice Beach Restaurant (Thong Nai Pan Yai)** — **the beachfront BBQ answer**. Nightly seafood BBQ with the day's whole fish displayed on ice; praised pad krapow. Casual, feet-in-sand. Budget ~150–400 THB/dish. **Verified** (travelmermaid.com, Feb 2026 update). ~5 min drive / 20 min beach-to-beach walk from Noi.
- **TNP Noi walking street** — ~20 restaurants + 7-Eleven/Tops Daily. Standouts (all verified via travelmermaid + thealwayswanderer 2026 guides): **Country E-ssan** (open-kitchen authentic Isaan, cheap), **Dawny Thaifood** (street pad thai 80 THB, near Buri Rasa), **That's Amore** (wood-fired pizza — safe kid pleaser), **Krua Thai** and **Monsoon** (well-rated Thai), **Banana Cafe** (healthy), **Brew Co. Cafe** (coffee).
- **Coco Loco (TNP Noi)** — coconut ice cream served with mango; two independent blogs call it out. Kids' after-dinner ritual sorted. **Verified** (2 sources).
- **55 Beach Kitchen / The Beach Club at Buri Rasa (Noi)** — long-standing beachfront restaurant-bar, nice but resort pricing (~3x street prices). **Verified** (siam2nite + travelmermaid).
- **2C Bar (far end of TNP Yai)** — multi-storey **treehouse-style viewpoint bar/cafe** built around huge trees, terraces over the bay; breakfast sets, shakes, Thai dishes (whole red snapper in tamarind sauce), open **10:00–21:00**. The best "viewpoint cafe" actually in TNP — steep access. **Verified** (thealwayswanderer, Mar 2026). This answers the "viewpoint cafe on the TNP road" question; no other specific viewpoint cafe on the access road could be verified.
- Note: **Valentino (Italian, Noi, Google 4.8/158)** is listed as **temporarily closed** (Wanderlog) — skip.

### (b) Chaloklum fishing-village dinner (~25–30 min drive, 14 km)
- **Standout: Phorn Restaurant With Seaview** — right at **Chaloklum pier**, Google **4.5 (405 reviews)**, moderate prices, "one of the best seafood noodle dishes," pier views. **Verified** (Wanderlog/Google data).
- **Nong Nook's Seafood** — village waterfront mom-&-pop, famous grilled red snapper, absurdly cheap (a big meal for two ≈ 300 THB). **Verified** (awaygowe.com guide + TripAdvisor presence).
- Also: **Sabaii Dee** (beachfront bamboo shack, BBQ seafood platter ~500 THB) and **Beach Garden** (nicer fusion/sushi, 150–1,500 THB) — both verified via thebackpackingfamily.com.
- **Timing gold: Chaloklum Sunday Walking Street — Sundays only, ~17:00–22:00** — you are on the island **Sunday 27.9**. Genuine local market at the fishing village: seafood straight off the boats, grilled meats, Thai sweets, crafts, live music, free entry. Combine market grazing + Phorn/Nong Nook dinner in one evening. **Verified** (thaiholidayguide.com).
- Clarification: the famous **Fisherman's Restaurant & Bar is in Baan Tai (south coast), NOT Chaloklum** — Google **4.7 (1,855 reviews)**, mid-range, lagoon-sunset tables, booking recommended. Worth knowing if you're near Thong Sala/Baan Tai at sunset (e.g., after Wat Pho sauna). **Verified** (Wanderlog).

### (c) West-coast sunset spots with kids (~40–50 min drive from TNP — make it a half-day)
- **Koh Raham (at Haad Son / "Secret Beach")** — cliffside driftwood bar/restaurant on a rock peninsula, swings, hammocks, steps straight into the sea for snorkeling (small fish + coral; bring reef shoes). **Entry free — you walk through Haad Son Resort; you just order food/drinks.** Arrive ~45–60 min before sunset (sunset ~18:15 early Oct). Caveats: steep, rough final access lane (park at top and walk); Sept–Oct seas can be rough and it occasionally closes. **Verified** (thaiholidayguide.com, Apr 2026 — two pages).
- **Amsterdam Bar (Wok Tum hillside, between Thong Sala and Sri Thanu)** — two-tier hillside deck + infinity pool, panoramic sunset. TripAdvisor **4.0 (1,047 reviews)**, $$–$$$; **pool use costs 100 THB**, drinks pricey; upper deck has the better view. Daily, sunset window ~17:00–20:00 — **arrive before 17:00** for seats. Family note: chilled reggae backpacker crowd; since cannabis legalization reviews mention joints — fine for a quick early-evening sunset with teens, but it's not a kids' venue. **Verified** (archived TripAdvisor page + siam2nite + TikTok).
- **Secret Beach Bar** — the bar on Haad Son beach itself below the same access; calm swimming, laid-back. Realistically you'd do **Koh Raham and Secret Beach as one stop**. **Verified** (thaiholidayguide.com).
- Practical: pick ONE west-coast sunset evening; roads back to TNP are dark and winding — drive back right after sunset.

### (d) Breakfast/brunch cafes worth the drive (pair with a Thong Sala/west-side morning)
- **Bubba's Coffee** — the island's brunch institution since 2015, own roastery; eggs Benedict, shakshuka, house-cured salmon toast, gluten-free/vegan options. Branches: **Baan Tai 7:00–17:00, Thong Sala 7:00–17:00, Roastery Haad Yao 8:00–16:30**. **Verified** (official bubbascoffee.com + phangan.events 2026 + multiple blogs). ~30–35 min from TNP.
- **Indigo Café (Sri Thanu; also Thong Sala, 7:00–22:30)** — smoothie bowls, healthy plates, bakery; the pick if you want the Sri Thanu "health cafe" scene. **Verified** (phangan.events, Mar 2026).
- **Nira's Home Bakery (Thong Sala, 7:00–17:00)** — legendary bakery breakfasts/cakes. **Verified** (phangan.events 2026).

### (e) Quirky extras you missed
- **Wat Pho Herbal Steam Sauna (Baan Tai)** — old-school temple sauna, lemongrass steam over a log fire since ~2002; separate men's/women's sections; **daily ~13:00–19:00, ~100–200 THB** (sources conflict: 100 THB per 2020 report, "around 200" per newer one — **price partially verified**). Genuinely quirky; teens will remember it. Combine with Fisherman's Baan Tai dinner.
- **Night squid fishing** — real and bookable: **Black Coral Tours** (departs Sri Thanu, private 17-m longtail, 5 hrs, life vests, keep your catch; **min 5,000 THB for 3, then 1,500 THB/person → ~10,500 THB for 7**; WhatsApp +66 62 728 2294, book 24h+ ahead). **Verified** (operator site). Cheaper local option: squid boats straight off **Chaloklum pier** (verified as a thing via gohobo.net; arrange on the spot — price unverified, expect ~800–1,500 THB pp).
- **Thong Sala Saturday Walking Street** ("Chinese street" by the post office) — **Saturdays ~16:00–22:00**, family-friendly local night market ~330 m from Phantip food market. You land on the island **Saturday 26.9** — do both in one first-night stroll. **Verified** (thaiholidayguide.com).
- **Retired Royal Navy ship at Thong Sala pier** — free, 20–30 min photo stop; honestly low kid-appeal, only if passing. **Verified**.

## 2. BANGKOK

### (a) Chinatown evening: Yaowarat + Talad Noi — YES, do it (fits Thu 1.10 or Fri 2.10 night)
- **Feasibility with 7:** very doable — it's grazing, not a sit-down. Go **18:00–20:30**; arrive via Grab (2 cars, ~10–15 min / 3–4 km from Chatrium) or MRT **Wat Mangkon**. **Key rule: many top stalls close Mondays** (street-vendor cleaning day) — your Thu/Fri slot is safe. **Verified** (willflyforfood.net stall-by-stall hours).
- **Verified stalls** (names, hours per willflyforfood.net):
  - **Guay Jub Ouan Pochana** (408 Yaowarat Rd) — peppery rolled-noodle pork soup, 11:00–24:00, closed Mon; famous queue, Michelin-guide territory.
  - **Werng Crab Fried Rice** — crab fried rice (the most kid-friendly plate in Chinatown), 17:30–23:00, closed Mon; 2023 Michelin street-food listing.
  - **Lim Lao Ngow** — 60-year-old flour-free fishball noodles, 16:30–20:30 daily.
  - **Nai Mong Hoi Tod** — crispy oyster/mussel omelette theatre, 10:00–19:00, closed Mon–Tue.
- Unverified but famous (label as "find on the night"): the **toasted-bun stall** (charcoal buns with custard/pandan dips — teen catnip) and the **T&K / Lek & Rut seafood corner** at Phadungdao junction. Budget overall ~100–200 THB/person to eat very well.
- **Talad Noi** (10-min walk toward the river): century-old Thai-Chinese alleys full of **street art, portrait murals and the famous rusted vintage-car photo spot** among motor-repair shops; 200-year-old **So Heng Tai mansion** (with indoor pool) and Chow Sue Kong shrine; **Photohostel & Photocafe** for a drink stop. 45–60 min stroll — do it at golden hour **before** eating Yaowarat. **Verified** (thesmartlocal.co.th).

### (b) Riverside near Chatrium: ICONSIAM SookSiam vs hotel
- **SookSiam (ICONSIAM, G floor) — yes, better than hotel dining for this crew.** Indoor "floating market": wooden boats on a real water channel, food vendors from **all 77 provinces** (khao soi, som tam, roti, seafood), most dishes cheap (~60–150 THB). **Open daily 10:00–22:00, free entry; free shuttle boat from Sathorn/Saphan Taksin pier every 10–15 min (~10-min crossing)**; from Chatrium take the hotel boat/BTS to Saphan Taksin first, or Grab (~15 min). Bonus: **free riverfront fountain-light show twice nightly ~19:00 and ~20:00**. **Verified** (official ICONSIAM/SookSiam + thaiholidayguide).
- Best slot: your free **Thursday arrival evening** (1.10) — dinner + fountain show, or attach to Sunday before the airport.

### (c) Dessert experience kids 10–14 rave about: **After You**
- Thailand's cult dessert chain (38 branches): **Shibuya honey toast** (butter-honey brick + ice cream, shareable) and **kakigori** (giant fluffy shaved-ice — Thai tea or mango sticky rice flavors). Expect ~200–300 THB per shared dessert (order 2–3 for 7 people; exact menu prices unverified).
- **Branch that fits your plan: Asiatique The Riverfront — daily 10:00–22:00** (tel 063-840-9792) → slot it into your **Saturday Asiatique evening** after Mahanakhon. **ICONSIAM branch, 6th floor, 10:00–21:00** → pairs with SookSiam. **Verified** (official branch pages via archive, 2023/2021 — hours may have shifted slightly; chain size verified 2026).

### (d) Sunday add-ons (Chatuchak / Lumpini)
- **Coconut ice cream in the shell at Chatuchak** — the market's classic; served in a coconut with toppings, stalls throughout food sections **6–8 and 26** (~40–60 THB, price unverified). Market runs Sat–Sun 9:00–18:00; go by 10:00 before the heat. **Verified as signature item** (thaiholidayguide).
- **Mixt Chatuchak mall** — air-con rescue **right next to the market**, daily 10:00–22:00, food court of relocated famous stalls (boat noodles, pad thai) + desserts. Perfect midday cool-down with 5 kids. **Verified** (thesmartlocal.co.th).
- **Children's Discovery Museum** is 476 m from the market (free) — probably young for your teens, but noted. **Lumpini Park**: the giant **monitor lizards** around the lake are the kid highlight (rent a pedal boat) — well known but unverified this session.

**Biggest wins for your exact schedule:** Sat 26.9 Thong Sala walking street + Phantip; **Sun 27.9 Chaloklum Sunday market + pier seafood dinner**; one west-coast afternoon = Koh Raham snorkel-sunset; one Bubba's/Indigo brunch morning; BKK Thu night SookSiam + fountain show; Fri night Talad Noi + Yaowarat; Sat After You at Asiatique; Sun coconut ice cream + Mixt at Chatuchak.
