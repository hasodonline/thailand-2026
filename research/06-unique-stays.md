# THAILAND FAMILY TRIP — UNIQUE STAYS REPORT
**Family of 7 (2 adults + 5 kids, ages 10–14) | Sept 18 – Oct 4, 2026 | Mid-range with selective splurges**

*Note on September pricing: this is green/low season everywhere on your route (and shoulder season on the Gulf islands), so rates below are realistic low-season estimates from current published rates — expect them to hold or improve for Sept 2026. THB rates marked ~ are estimates from USD listings at ~35 THB/USD.*

## 5. KOH PHANGAN — Thong Nai Pan (4 nights, Sep 28–Oct 2)

## CROSS-TRIP BOOKING STRATEGY

1. **Book first (capacity-constrained):** Chiang Dao Nest (email direct, ~20 cabins), Pai Island Resort (10 villas), Rabeang Pasak (9 treehouses), Reverie Siam if chosen (20 rooms), Rasananda 2BR villa if chosen (single-category). Do these as soon as dates are firm — ideally by May–June 2026.
2. **Book 2–3 months out:** Santhiya/Buri Rasa interconnecting requests, Chiang Mai pool villa, Phu Chaisai.
3. **Safe to book last:** The Riverie, Chatrium, Belle Villa, Azalea — big inventory, green season, refundable rates.
4. **Always take refundable rates** in Sept — green-season discounts on flexible rates are already deep; non-refundable saves only ~10–15%.
5. **Rough nightly totals for 7, following the #1 picks:** Chiang Mai villa ~THB 10k • Chiang Dao Nest ~THB 3.5k • Pai Island ~THB 12k • Riverie ~THB 8.5k • Santhiya ~THB 13k • Chatrium ~THB 10k → a trip average around **THB 9,500–10,500/night** for genuinely memorable places, with the option to swing the splurge (Rasananda 2BR villa) or save (Belle Villa Pai) by ±THB 15k total.
