# Optional Cool Stops — Chiang Mai Province, 19–22 Sep 2026

## Day 1 — Sat 19.9: Wua Lai / Saturday Walking Street evening

**Important general finding:** almost every famous khao soi shop in Chiang Mai closes 14:00–17:00. Evening khao soi = either the market stalls or Huen Phen's dinner session.

### Dinner
1. **Huen Phen** ✅ — 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Old City (700 m / 8–10 min walk north of the Wua Lai market). THE 60-year Lanna institution: day room serves khao soi, the **evening session (17:00–22:00 daily, confirmed by 2 sources)** is a candlelit, antique-crammed northern-Thai dinner — khao soi, sai ua sausage, hang lay pork curry, khao kan jin. Kids' win: it looks like eating inside a museum of weird old stuff. Budget ~150–300 THB/person. Google ~4.4/5 (2,700+ reviews via SawadeeChiangMai); TripAdvisor lower (3.6) mostly complaining about tourist crowds. Walk over ~17:30 before the market peaks, or eat late.
2. **Khao soi at the market itself** ✅ — khao soi stalls are confirmed present along Wua Lai (CK Travels + Feel Chiang Mai guides), 40–60 THB a bowl, plus crocodile skewers, okonomiyaki-on-a-stick, flame-grilled cheese jacket potatoes (80 THB) — a "grazing dinner" the kids will prefer anyway. Best variety: stalls near Wat Sri Suphan (middle of the street) and the first food court on the right entering from Chiang Mai Gate.
3. *(Skip for evening)* Khao Soi Islam ✅ closes 17:00; Khao Soi Lung Prakit closes 16:30 — see Day 2 hack below.

### Dessert
- **The coconut ice cream lady + market sweets** ✅ — the coconut-shell ice cream stall inside the Saturday market is famous enough to have TripAdvisor reviews titled "Coconut ice-cream is to die for!" and "worth it for the little old lady". ~30–50 THB. Also mango sticky rice and coconut pancakes throughout.
- **Banana roti at Pratu Chiang Mai (South Gate) night market** ✅ — the nightly food market directly across from the Wua Lai entrance; hot Nutella-banana roti made to order, ~30–60 THB. Two dessert stops, zero extra walking.

Market hours ✅: ~16:30–22:00; arrive by 17:00–17:30 for space and daylight.

## Day 2 — Sun 20.9: Hotel → Grand Canyon (Hang Dong) → Lannawild (Huai Kaeo, Mae On)

### Morning hack (ON route)
- **Khao Soi Lung Prakit Kad Kom** ✅ — 53 Suriyawong 5, Hai Ya — **3 min drive from the Wua Lai area, on the way out of town toward Hang Dong**. Michelin Bib Gourmand, featured on Netflix's *Somebody Feed Phil*, beef khao soi ~60 THB, Google 4.4/5 (3,700+ reviews). **Opens 09:30** — a legendary 60-baht second-breakfast en route to the water park. 20–30 min. Detour: +0 min.

### After Grand Canyon (14:30 departure, heading east)
- **Ginger Farm** ✅⚠️ — organic rice farm in **Saraphi** district, which sits right on the ring-road drive from Hang Dong to Mae On (detour ~10 min). Hours Mon–Fri 10:00–19:00 (Sun 9:00–20:00). Kids activities: rice planting/harvesting, buffalo & pig bathing, egg collecting, craft workshops, and a **giant mud slide with life jackets — sessions 11:30 and 15:30** (chiangmaikids.com). The 15:30 mud-slide session fits: arrive ~15:00, slide 15:30–16:00, reach Lannawild ~17:00 (≈15 min later than planned). Northern-Thai farm café on site. ⚠️ Activity prices "on request" — book via phone +66 52 004 473 / Line before; blogs suggest a few hundred THB per child. Note: they've been mid-water-park all day, so mud may be redundant — treat as the "energy leftover" option.
- **San Kamphaeng Hot Springs** ✅ — Mae On district, ~5 min off route 1317 shortly before the Huai Kaeo turn — genuinely ON the route. Daily 07:00–18:00. Foreigner fee 100 THB adult / 50 THB child (+~50 THB mineral pool, 200–300 THB private tubs). The kid magnets: a **geyser jetting up to 20 m** and **buying baskets of eggs to boil in the spring channels (~15 min), eaten streamside with salt**. 45–60 min stop. Fits 15:30–16:20 if they skip Ginger Farm.
- **Muang On Cave (Tham Muang On)** ✅ — 5 min from the hot springs, same valley. 30 THB/person, daily 08:00–17:00. Naga staircase up, long concrete stairway down into a huge chamber with a 30-ft glowing stalagmite. Cool and quirky, but note there IS a large reclining Buddha inside — if that reads as "temple" to the kids, skip. Steep stairs; 40–60 min. Realistically choose hot springs **or** cave, not both, to hit Lannawild by ~17:00.

### Mae Kampong village cluster (10–20 min from Lannawild — best done Monday morning BEFORE Skyline Luge, or late Sunday if energy allows)
- **The Giant Chiangmai treehouse cafe** ✅ status / ⚠️ hours — Ban Pok, **Huai Kaeo, Mae On — the same sub-district as Lannawild**, ~10 min drive. **CONFIRMED OPEN**: Google reviews run through 25 Jan 2026, rating 4.0/5 (2,947 reviews, via Wanderlog). Cafe built into a massive banyan tree with swinging suspension bridges over the jungle and a **short zipline between platforms** (July 2025 blog visit confirms zipline operating). Coffee/snacks cheaper than city cafes; typical stay ~45 min. ⚠️ Hours unpublished (commonly ~8:00–17:00) and the access road is **rough and steep — avoid after rain**; call +66 86 776 2946 the day before. No reservation reportedly needed for the cafe. This is the single quirkiest stop of the whole area for these kids.
- **Mae Kampong Waterfall** ✅ — free, 10–15 min walk from the village center (or park nearby, ~20 THB). Seven tiers, fern-and-moss banks, wooden stairs climbing beside the falls. 30–45 min. Note one tour operator says the last stretch of road needs a local shuttle (~150 THB pp) for low-clearance cars ⚠️.
- **Village stroll** ✅ — no entry fee; coffee 40–60 THB, dishes 60–100 THB, stilt houses over the stream. 1–2 hours total with the waterfall.
- **Chom Nok Chom Mai cafe** ⚠️ UNVERIFIED — I could not confirm this cafe's current status/hours from any reachable source today. Don't plan around it; the village has plenty of stream-side cafes to improvise with.

## Day 3 — Mon 21.9: Skyline Luge (Mae On) → Mae Sa Valley (Mae Rim)

The cross-town leg (118 → outer ring 121 → 107) has no standout family stops — better to bank the time for Mae Sa. In the valley itself (all strung along route 1096, i.e., ON route or minutes off it):

- **Elephant PooPooPaper Park** ✅ — Mae Raem, Mae Rim, ~5 min off 1096. **Daily 9:00–17:00, last guided tour 16:00** (official site) — so their ~15:00 arrival works: do it 15:15–16:30. Guided outdoor tour of turning elephant dung into paper, then a DIY studio where each kid makes their own poop-paper souvenir; playground + treehouse cafe on site. Featured by Lonely Planet/UNESCO/WWF. ⚠️ Admission not posted on site; historically ~100 THB self-guided / ~500 THB with full workshop — confirm at gate (TripAdvisor page blocked today). For this exact crew — "paper made of poo" — probably the highest laugh-per-baht stop of the trip. 60–75 min.
- **X-Centre** ✅ — 816 Moo 1, Mae Rim–Samoeng Rd (route 1096, ON route, minutes from Mae Sa Valley Craft Village). Activities 9:00–18:00 daily: **go-karts (800 THB / 15 min, helmet + insurance included)**, buggies/UTV, ATV, paintball, bungee, kids' activities; restaurant on site 8:00–18:00. ⚠️ Age/height limits not published — call 053-297700. Could absorb the late afternoon if PooPooPaper doesn't.
- **Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden canopy walkway** ✅ — on 1096. Daily 8:30–17:00; foreigners 100 THB adult / 50 THB child, **400 m glass-and-steel canopy walk 20 m above the forest included free**; internal shuttle 30/10 THB. 1–1.5 hr. Nature-loving kids will like it; it closes 17:00, so it competes with PooPooPaper — pick one for today.
- **Mae Sa Waterfall** ✅ — 10-tier waterfall just off 1096. Foreigners 100 THB adult / 50 THB child; 2.5 km forest trail along the tiers. ⚠️ National-park hours ~8:00–16:30/17:00 and swimming is traditionally allowed at the lower/mid tiers, but I could not re-verify the swimming rule today. September = full wet-season flow: spectacular but currents can close swimming.
- **Mon Cham (Mon Jam) viewpoint** ⚠️ for evening — 30–40 min further up steep switchbacks from Mae Sa. Ridge and viewpoints free; flower field ~40 THB; Nong Hoi Royal Project area 07:00–18:00. It's really a **morning sea-of-mist place** — sources emphasize pre-9AM, not sunset — and the road up is steep/tight, unpleasant in September rain and in the dark coming down. My call: **skip on this trip** unless the evening is bone-dry and everyone's fresh.

### Dinner near Mae Sa Valley Craft Village
- **Mae Sa Valley Craft Village's own restaurant** — simplest (they're sleeping there); confirm serving hours at check-in.
- **Steak of the Day** ⚠️ hours — Mae Rim Plaza, Rim Tai (~10 min back toward Mae Rim town). Google 4.5/5, chef-driven Thai + Western daily menu — the "kids want something not-spicy tonight" insurance policy. Evening hours not confirmed today; check Google Maps before driving.

## Day 4 — Tue 22.9: Mae Sa Valley → (city western bypass, canal road/121) → Chai Lai Orchid (Mae Wang)

The 121 bypass runs Mae Rim → Mae Hia → Hang Dong — these stops are literally on the line:

- **Huay Tung Tao Lake** ✅ — off the canal road, ~10 min after leaving Mae Rim heading south, +5 min detour. Entry 50 THB foreigner / 20 THB Thai (kids often waved through), 8:00–18:00. **Giant straw sculptures** (the famous King Kong family and friends, rebuilt seasonally ⚠️ — current lineup unconfirmed), bamboo dining huts ON the water (hut ~20 THB, grilled salt-crusted fish + papaya salad 300–500 THB feeds 2–3), pedal boats ~80 THB/30 min. **Verdict: do it TODAY** — it's exactly on this morning's route; only save it for Thursday if Thursday's plan already passes the canal road (they're near-identical detours; don't do it twice).
- **Royal Park Rajapruek** ✅ — Mae Hia, right on the bypass. Daily 8:00–18:00; foreigners 200 THB adult / 150 THB child (100–140 cm; the kids are likely above 140 → adult-ish rates possible — bring passports? unclear, fee is height-based). Big formal royal gardens + tram. Honestly: beautiful but slow-paced and garden-y — **weak fit for these kids**; only if it pours and they need a paved stroll. 1.5–2 hr.
- **Baan Kang Wat artist village** ⚠️ — Suthep/Mae Hia edge, ~10 min off the bypass. Free entry, craft shops and tiny cafes in wooden houses; generally ~10:00–18:00 and **closed Mondays** (so Tuesday is fine), but hours page was unreachable today. 45 min. Nice, not essential.

### Lunch before Chai Lai (arriving ~13:30)
- **Chom Cafe & Restaurant** ✅ — Mae Hia, ON the bypass (Somphot 700 Pi Rd), **daily 11:00–22:00**, Google 4.3/5 (4,494 reviews), ~200–400 THB pp. The famous **misty "fairy waterfall" garden seating** — mist machines, moss, koi, waterfall walls. Kids get a jungle-movie lunch; timing is perfect (pass ~11:30–12:00). This beats squeezing it into Sunday morning (it opens at 11:00, too late that day).
- **Alternative: eat AT Chai Lai Orchid** ✅⚠️ — its riverside restaurant/coffee shop (listed as a public feature on TripAdvisor) overlooks the Mae Wang river with **elephants wandering past the tables**. Since check-in is 13:30 anyway, a late lunch there is the laziest good option. Menu hours unpublished — but as a lodge restaurant it serves through the afternoon.

## Biggest takeaways for the parents
1. **Book/confirm nothing-day-of items by phone**: The Giant (+66 86 776 2946, road condition!), Ginger Farm mud-slide session (+66 52 004 473), X-Centre kart age limits (053-297700).
2. **Khao soi is a daytime dish** — the two "real" hits are Lung Prakit Sunday 09:30 (on route!) and Huen Phen's 17:00–22:00 dinner room Saturday.
3. **Monday 15:00–17:00 is one slot with three contenders** (PooPooPaper / QSBG canopy / X-Centre) — for these specific kids, PooPooPaper first, X-Centre if time remains, canopy walk drops.
4. September rain caveat applies to: The Giant's access road, Mon Cham switchbacks, and swimming at Mae Sa Falls.
